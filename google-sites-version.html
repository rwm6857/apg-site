HOW APG WORKS

One voice, four goals, seven ways we deliver

Autism Providers of Georgia is a 501(c)(6) professional association that serves and advocates for autism therapy providers across the state, to protect and strengthen access to quality care for Georgians on the autism spectrum.

Fair & sustainable reimbursement
Transparent regulatory processes
Stable Medicaid CMO policies
Quality care for Georgia families

What we do for our members

01 Legislative monitoring & advocacy
We track bills, resolutions and budget provisions before the General Assembly.
02 Medicaid policy & CMO monitoring
We monitor DCH actions and CMO contract and rate changes.
03 Regulatory & agency engagement
We submit formal comments to DCH and other agencies.
04 Provider data coordination
We survey members to document the impact of policy and payer actions.
05 Coalition & stakeholder relations
We build relationships with allies, legislators and stakeholders.
06 Testimony & public communications
We prepare testimony and talking points for hearings and comment periods.
07 Member communications & alerts
We send legislative summaries, regulatory alerts and CMO transition updates.
