Georgia agrees to a 3% increase for ABA services

Georgia has agreed to a 3% rate increase for ABA services. It's a first step toward our broader goal: reimbursement that reflects the real cost of delivering care.

GAMMIS now processes with BCBA certification alone

In a recent meeting with the MCO director, APG raised the credentialing and licensure challenges affecting members. GAMMIS now shows a banner stating applications "can be processed using only the submitted certification" (the BCBA certification). There are two upload points, one for certification and one for license. Providers who have not yet applied for licensure may upload the certificate to both. Once licensure is obtained, submit a Change of Information Form.
